How to Use Cruzo

Multi-Device Local Wireless Audio in 4 simple steps

1

Connect to Same Local Network

Ensure the Host PC and all friends' phones are connected to the same Wi-Fi router or laptop mobile hotspot. 0 MB internet data is used—all audio travels strictly over local LAN.

2

Start Host Audio Broadcast (PC)

On your laptop, go to Host Broadcast (/host) and click "Start Audio Broadcast". Choose Entire Screen (or your movie tab) and check "Also share system audio".

3

Join as Listener (Phones)

Friends scan the on-screen QR code (or visit the displayed local URL). Tap the large "TAP TO UNMUTE & START AUDIO" button to enable mobile audio playback through their Bluetooth earbuds.

4

Dial in Lip-Sync & Bluetooth Delay

Bluetooth earbuds usually introduce 120ms–200ms delay. Adjust the Personal Bluetooth Sync slider on your phone so sound perfectly aligns with the actors' lips on the laptop screen.

Tips & Shortcuts:
  • In VLC: Press J on the PC keyboard 3–4 times to shift audio -150ms ahead for the entire group.
  • iPhone Users: Ensure your physical side mute/silent switch is turned OFF.
  • Screen Lock: Cruzo includes an active audio wake-lock so playback continues in the background.
